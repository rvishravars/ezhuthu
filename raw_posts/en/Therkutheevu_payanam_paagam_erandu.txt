Private flight training institutes in Oamaru are very popular - especially among Indians. When researching the reason for this on Google, I considered the low air traffic over Oamaru as one of the reasons. Even at the pizza shop, you can occasionally spot future pilots.

The person who cooked and served our pizza was a newly joined employee of Indian origin - wearing a turban, he was a new hire. Indians living in New Zealand do not usually exchange big greetings when they see people from their country of birth. Sometimes it seems to me like some of them are trying to tell me something with their eyes.

We decided to take the pizza in Oamaru and eat it in the car. I parked the car at a rest stop about fifteen minutes from Oamaru. Our stop was next to the continuously flowing Waitaki River. Beyond the river is the Canterbury region. The Waitaki River is a major braided river, 209 kilometers (130 miles) long, located in New Zealand's South Island; it drains from the Mackenzie Basin, flows through three hydroelectric dams, and empties into the Pacific Ocean. Looking at the Google map, its braided shape can be seen. This river is also famous for recreational fishing.

It is a winding road from Dunedin to Oamaru. Then to Timaru, there aren't that many curves - except for the braids of the Waitaki River. The Pacific Ocean to the east, and to the west, defining the Canterbury and North Otago plains, are flat or gently sloping pastures, crop fields, and livestock fences.

If I drive the car to the west - rugged inland roads - that was a desire that arose in my mind. I made a mental note of these routes to travel again on my motorcycle. For me, traveling with my daughter in a small car, the smooth Timaru highway was suitable. The two of us rested for a while in Timaru. Timaru is a port city. Here in 1903, even before the Wright brothers, Richard Pearse supposedly made an unconfirmed powered flight. Sitting in the car, I learned about this curly-haired inventor on the internet.

As soon as she ate the pizza in Oamaru, my daughter, embraced by sleep, woke up suddenly as if she was floating in the sky in her dream and saw a thunderstorm.
